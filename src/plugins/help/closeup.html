JS9 Close-up

The Close-up plugin, available from the Analysis menu, opens a resizable window showing a magnified view of the display centered on the mouse position. It updates live as the mouse moves over the image.

The pixel under the cursor is outlined in green and pointed at by tick marks from the edges of the window. Underneath the view, a readout gives the image coordinates and value of that pixel, plus its WCS position when the image has a valid WCS.

The magnification defaults to eight display pixels per close-up pixel. It can be changed with the command buttons at the top of the window, or by scrolling the mouse wheel over the close-up itself. Because the view is taken from the display, it reflects the current colormap, scale, contrast/bias, flip and rotation, and it overlays regions in the same way the Magnifier plugin does.

Defaults are set in the JS9.CloseUp.opts object: zoom (initial magnification), minZoom and maxZoom, markerColor, background, and regions (whether to overlay the regions layer).
